How to request deletion

  1. Fill in the form below

    Enter the phone number or email you used to sign up, so we can locate your account.

  2. Send the request

    Tapping "Send deletion request" opens your email app with a pre-filled message addressed to taxdoctorspk@gmail.com. Just hit send.

  3. We verify it's you

    We may reply to confirm the request matches the account on file, to prevent someone else from deleting your account.

  4. Your data is deleted

    We process the request and confirm by email once it's complete — typically within 7 business days.
